📖 How to Play
  • Each floor has 10 rooms with random events
  • ⚔️ Monsters: Battle NPC enemies — defeat them to loot their Monster item
  • 📦 Chests: Everyone gets unique loot based on chest tier
  • 🪤 Traps: Negative stat effects for the whole party (duration shown in rooms)
  • ⛩️ Shrines: Positive stat buffs for matching pets (duration shown in rooms)
  • 👹 Boss (Room 9): Tough boss fight — rewards Keys + XP, no Monster loot
  • 🎁 Reward (Room 10): Always a guaranteed chest for the whole party
  • All party members must click "Continue" to progress

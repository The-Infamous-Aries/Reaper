📖 How to Play
  • 🗺️ Dungeon Structure: Each floor has 10 rooms with random events. Room 9 is always a Boss, Room 10 is Floor Loot.
  • 🎭 Dungeon Types: Choose from 12 themed locations — these are thematic only and don't provide gameplay bonuses.
  • Event Types You'll Encounter:
    Monster - Battle enemies
    Story - Narrative choices
    Puzzle - Solve challenges
    Merchant - Buy items
    Chest - Free loot
    Trap - Escape or endure
    Shrine - Get buffs
    Boss - Room 9 fight
  • Monster Encounters: Fight, Scare, or Flee from enemies. Battles drop Monster items and XP.
  • Story Segments: AI-generated narrative challenges with 3 skill-based choices testing different stat combinations.
  • Puzzles: Solve environmental puzzles using your pet's INT, DEX, and ENE stats.
  • Merchant: Buy ANY pet item using your accumulated XP as currency. XP persists across the dungeon.
  • Chests/Mimics: Carefully approach chests or fight them. Mimics disguise as chests but drop double loot when defeated!
  • Traps: Escape using stats or accept damage. Failed escapes give 1-hour cooldowns before retry.
  • Shrines: Gain positive buffs (ATT/DEF/DEX/INT boost). Works for all pets regardless of element/type.
  • Boss (Room 9): Powerful boss with AI-generated lore. Drops Keys only (no chests).
  • Floor Loot (Room 10): Guaranteed scaled loot chest. Claiming it gives 1-hour cooldown before accessing the next floor.
  • ⏰ Cooldowns: Failed rooms require 1-hour wait before retry. Completing Room 10 gives 1-hour floor cooldown.
  • 💰 XP System: Earn XP from completing rooms (based on floor, room, event type). Spend it at Merchants or keep accumulating!

What Are Pets?

Type and Element Advantages

Pets have types and elements that give them advantages in battles. Understanding these is key to victory! The bonuses from Type and Element advantages are multiplicative. For example, if a pet has both a Type advantage (15%) and an Element advantage (10%), the total damage bonus would be 26.5% (1.15 * 1.10 = 1.265).


Each pet has one Type (either Land, Swimming, or Flying) and can have one or two Elements. Pets with the Basic element are an exception; they can only have that single element and do not receive a second one. While normal elements grant a 10% damage bonus when they have an advantage, the Basic element is neutral and has a slight 10% damage disadvantage against all other elements, making it a challenging choice.

Excluding the Basic element, you can create a pet from 3 Types and 12 Elements. A pet can have one element (36 combinations) or two different elements (198 combinations), leading to a total of 234 unique Type and Element combinations!

Leveling Up and Equipment Multipliers

Your pet grows stronger by leveling up and equipping powerful items.

  • Leveling Up: Pets gain experience (XP) from various activities. The XP needed to level up increases with each level, following the formula: 200 * (1.03 ^ (level - 1)). When a pet levels up, they gain 5 stat points, randomly distributed.
  • Equipment Multipliers: Equipment provides significant stat boosts.
    • Level Milestone Bonus: For every 50 levels your pet has, the stats from all equipped items are increased by a multiplier. At level 50, the multiplier is 2x. At level 100, it's 3x, and so on.
    • Duplication Bonus: If you equip two of the exact same Material, Gem, or Monster, the stat bonuses from that pair of items are doubled.

Loot System

You can find a variety of items to power up your pet. Keys are frequently looted from most winning encounters and commands. These keys are used to open chests in the loot market, each offering different amounts of loot.

  • Item Tiers: Items like Materials, Gems, Monsters, Potions, and Hats come in different rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Mythic.
  • Finding Loot: You can get loot from missions, battles, and opening chests. The higher the difficulty of the activity, the better your chances of getting rare loot.
  • Inventory Limits: Your pet has limited inventory space for each item. You can hold up to 16 of each Potion, and 5 of each other type of item (Hats, Materials, etc.). Any excess items are automatically converted into XP.

Equipment Slots

Your pet has 7 equipment slots to customize their stats.

  • Hat: 1 Slot
  • Materials: 2 Slots
  • Gems: 2 Slots
  • Monsters: 2 Slots

The equipment layout is: Monster, Gem, Material, Hat, Material, Gem, Monster.

Pet Battles

Test your pet's strength in various battle modes.

  • PvE (Player vs. Environment): Use the /battle command to fight against a randomly generated monster. You can choose from Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.
  • PvP (Player vs. Player): Challenge other players to a duel! You can start a 1v1 fight or a Free-For-All (FFA) lobby with up to 9 other players using the /pvp command.
  • Tournaments: Create or join a tournament with 4, 8, or 16 players using the /tournament command to see who is the ultimate pet master.

Pet Activities

There are many ways to interact with your pet and earn rewards. Below is a summary of the main activities.

Activity Command Description
Play /play A simple, low-risk activity to earn a small amount of XP and find keys. The outcome is influenced by your pet's elements and the location you choose to play in. There is a small chance of a boss encounter.
Train /train A training session with a decent chance of success for a moderate XP gain. Failure might provoke a battle with a wild pet. This activity has a short cooldown.
Mission /mission A risky but rewarding activity. It has a lower success rate than training but offers much greater XP rewards and a better chance for keys. You can gamble your own XP for a bigger payout on success, but you'll lose it on failure. Failure can also trigger a wild pet encounter. This activity has a medium cooldown.
Quest /quest Embark on a story-driven, multi-stage adventure. Your choices, tested against your pet's stats (like ATT, INT, DEX), determine the outcome, rewards, and the path of the story.
Survivor Series /survivor series A challenging battle royale mode where your pet competes against a large group of other pets. The last one standing is crowned the champion. XP is awarded based on the number of eliminations your pet achieves during the series.

Pet Casino

Feeling lucky? Take your pet to the casino to win (or lose) big on XP!

Activity Command Description
Races /race Bet on your pet in a race against others. A pet's speed is determined by its stats (DEX, ENE, HAP).
Slots /slots Spin the slot machine to match emojis. Higher difficulties offer bigger rewards. The 'Insanity' mode provides a unique challenge based on your pet's attributes.
Blackjack /blackjack The classic card game where the goal is to get a hand value as close to 21 as possible without exceeding it, beating the dealer's hand.
Craps /craps A dice game with various betting options on the outcome of a roll. Players bet on the 'shooter' to win or lose.
Hold'em /holdem The classic Texas Hold'em poker game. Make the best five-card hand using your two private cards and five community cards.

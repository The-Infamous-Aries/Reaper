The Setup: Your pet gets yeeted into a 13-zone elemental arena — Fire, Ice, Necro, Holy, and nine other places no sane pet would vacation. They don't get a map. They don't get a choice. They get a vibe and a prayer.

🗺️ Every 15 minutes, a round fires. Pets roam zones based on their relationships: they chase Enemies like they have a personal vendetta, cling to Best Friends like a security blanket, avoid Friends (it's complicated), and ignore Foes while pretending they're not scared.

⚔️ Combat score = Survive Score (level ÷ multiplier ÷ 10) × element & type matchups × Charge bonus. Each matchup advantage stacks ×1.2, each disadvantage ×0.8 — across up to 5 axes (type triangle + 4 element combos). Higher score wins. Upsets happen. Cope.

⚡ Charge: Every round your pet skips combat, they build a Charge stack (+×0.15 per stack, caps at ×2.2 after 8 stacks). It resets the moment they fight. Abilities can raise the cap further and grant starting stacks. Hiding is a valid strategy — until it isn't.

🤝 Deals & Pressure: If nobody dies for a few rounds, the arena shrinks and desperate pets start forming shady truces with their enemies to gang up on someone worse. The arena does not reward cowardice.

💀 Elimination is permanent. Dead is dead. Your pet will not be consulted, appealed to, or given a participation trophy.

🏆 Winning: Last pet standing takes the crown. Up to 4 mutual Best Friends can share the win if they're the last ones standing — 60% chance for 2, 40% for 3, 25% for 4, or guaranteed if they pulled off a group elimination together. Everyone else gets nothing and has to think about what they did.

⚠️ Once you join, there is no leaving. Your pet has accepted their fate even if you haven't.

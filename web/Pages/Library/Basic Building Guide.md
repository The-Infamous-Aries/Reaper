### **Basic Nation Building Guide**

In most cases a nation would stay at c1 until grinding out 400m ($400,000,000) to make the jump to c20. Upon making the jump to c20 you will be met with a choice

* Raid and Warmonger  
* Farm for Resources

However despite being able to sorta farm the real question is if you want to raid the active or just beige snipe targets.

* Raiding active targets requires much lower infrastructure and not being scared to take hits back but is very fun, fast paced gameplay and you make sorta decent money if done right  
  * This minimum infra is 950   
    * This allows you to run a 5/5/5/3 military build with 1 power plant.   
  * The maximum infra is 1300   
    * This allows you to keep the 5/5/5/3 military build, 1 power plant, 3 Stadiums and 4 Shopping Malls  
    * The reason for keeping small amounts of infra is so you don't lose much and can bounce back & the only reason to add commerce is to balance out your daily military cost.  
    * **PLEASE NOTE\!** Despite being able to keep more that 950 the **REAL** max here is **950**

* Beige sniping requires more patience and also decent timing but this is a much slower gameplay, you lose pretty much nothing and make very good money when catching the right targets  
  * The minimum infra is 1250  
    * This allows you to keep a 5/0/0/1 military build, 1 power plant, 10 stacked mines, 1 Subway, 3 Stadiums and 4 Shopping Malls.  
    * Tho depending on alliance your sniping from you might want more military and less Mines & Commerce  
  * There is no real maximum here but keep the following in mind  
    * Only use Uranium power plants to keep improvement slots for more valuable improvements  
    * Stacking mines to 10 (Uranium is 5\) gives you the max bonus and farms stack to 20 for max  
    * Manufacturing stacks to 5 for max bonus  
    * The more infra you keep the higher the cost to rebuild if countered or attacked 

Despite the route you take or way you play there are a few key things to take into consideration

* Get to at least 4000 land in each city for more population and revenue  
  * If at any time you're blocked and don't want to be raided for your cash, BUY LAND\!  
    * You never lose land  
* Nukes cost a ton to make so unless your gonna take a **TON** of high level infra with one don't waste the money just send missiles  
  * I strongly recommend **NOT** making or stockpiling either since they raise your score, cost a lot for upkeep and can be spied away 1 day after creation  
    * Pretty much make and send as needed  
* The quickest way to end a raid is 5 Naval attacks & 3 Ground attacks  
  * 5 Naval attacks \= 70 Resistance  
    * If they have no vessels you can send 1 for immense triumph  
  * 3 Ground attacks \= 30 Resistance  
    * Do **NOT** use tanks or munitions unless needed for immense triumph  
* At no point in time do you have to raid but until you reach about c30 you will not be making enough resources or revenue to grow without waiting long periods or taking massive loans.

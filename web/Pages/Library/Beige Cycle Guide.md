### **How to PROPERLY beige cycle**

* Beige cycling is when 2 people hit the same target and reduce resistance to **JUST BEFORE 0**.   
  * **DO NOT** end till a 3rd has joined  
  * For strategic purposes it's best to have the 3rd join the **SAME** turn 1 of the first 2 end


* At this time a 3rd person would attack the same target and **ONLY 1** of the first 2 would end the war (the second would let it expire).   
  * If both end the war then the target has to many beige turns for the 3rd to wait out


* The 3rd person who hit the target can now reduce infra to **JUST BEFORE 0** and wait for the target to be automatically dropped from beige.   
  * It's better to slowly drain resistance at this point to avoid them double buying units and retaliate 


* At this time the target leaves beige 2 more can attack and the 3rd person from the first attack round can now beige the target.   
  * Like before it's best if both the new attackers join and the previous attacker ends in the same turn


* The 2 new attackers can reduce resistance to **JUST BEFORE 0** and wait for the target to auto pop from beige and a new 3rd attack can hit allowing **ONLY 1** of the first 2 to beige and the other would let it expire.   
  * At any point when the target is in beige it is best to **SLOWLY** drain resistance to make sure you don't get hit with double purchased units.  
  * It's less risky when there are 2 but still be cautious.

* You would repeat this over and over until the target is submissive or they delete their nation.  
  * Or until instructed to stop by alliance leadership.

# The FAFO Doctrine

## “To those who seek peace, I extend my hand.

## To those who test my resolve, I extend my blade.”

---

# Preamble

The **FAFO Doctrine** is the unshakable principle by which **Aries** defines his presence. It is not a suggestion, nor is it a policy subject to the whims of an alliance—it is an absolute law. Aries stands as the sole **Keeper of this Doctrine**. While he honors diplomacy and respects the sovereignty of his peers, he does not cower, and he does not forget.

This doctrine serves as both a warning and a promise: Regardless of tags, titles, or ties, those who provoke Aries or endanger what he protects will face the full weight of his reckoning. **If you Fuck Around, you WILL Find Out.**

---

# Article I: The Principle of Measured Response

Aries does not hunt for conflict, but he is its master when found. Any act of aggression, espionage, or betrayal will be met with a swift, calculated, and personal retaliation.

The response is always proportional to the offense, governed by the **FAFO Curve**. A minor slight may earn a cycle of discomfort; a grave betrayal ensures a permanent end.

## The Functional Calculus of Consequence

In this architecture, the **Y-axis** represents the magnitude of the "Find Out," while the **X-axis** represents the stupidity of the "Fuck Around."

* **X-Axis (Fuck Around):** As an individual increases their level of reckless or provocative behavior, they move further into Aries' sights.  
* **Y-Axis (Find Out):** The vertical climb represents the severity of the consequence.  
* **Exponential Growth:** Aries does not react linearly. Small breaches in respect lead to disproportionately large repercussions to ensure the lesson is never required a second time.

---

# Article II: The Autonomy of the Keeper

Aries’ blade is not owned by any syndicate, nor is his hand stayed by the signature of a diplomat.

* **Neutrality of Enforcement:** No alliance shield or treaty provides sanctuary from this Doctrine. If a "friend" Fucks Around, they will Find Out with the same certainty as an enemy.  
* **Protection of Stakes:** Aries chooses whom he shields. Those under his personal protection carry his seal; an attack on them is an attack on the Doctrine itself.  
* **Diplomatic Failure:** While Aries may allow space for words, if diplomacy fails to rectify a slight, the Doctrine takes precedence over peace.

---

# Article III: The Cost of Betrayal

Betrayal is the highest form of "Fucking Around."

* **Internal Treason:** Any who claim to stand with Aries but sow discord or act against his values will be excised with surgical finality.  
* **Broken Pacts:** Any power that violates a trust or breaks a word given to Aries will find that trust replaced by fire.  
* **No Negotiation:** Betrayal is a terminal offense. There is no bargaining once the curve has reached its peak.

---

# Article IV: The Inviolable Name

The reputation of Aries is both a shield and a weapon. Slander is not ignored; it is answered.

* **Exposure:** Any attempt to tarnish the name of the Keeper through deceit will be brought to light.  
* **Action over Words:** The world must understand that Aries does not bluff. When the threshold is crossed, action is the only remaining language.

---

# Conclusion

The FAFO Doctrine is a declaration of identity. Aries will never strike without cause, but he will never hesitate to finish what another has started. His loyalty is to the Principle, his dignity is untouchable, and his strength is a constant.

To those who seek friendship, Aries offers a steady hand. To those who seek to test him, he offers a lesson written in steel:

**Fuck Around and Find Out\!**

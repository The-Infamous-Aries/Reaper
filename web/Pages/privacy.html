🌙 Privacy Policy 🌙

Last Updated: May 25, 2026

"We collect souls, not secrets. But we still need to tell you about the data thing."

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Privacy Policy of Reaper Bot. This document explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data. We take your privacy seriously—almost as seriously as we take pet battles and war declarations. (Okay, maybe not THAT seriously, but we do care.)

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Discord User Information

Through Discord's API, we collect:

  • User ID: Your unique Discord identifier (we need this to know who you are)
  • Username & Discriminator: Your display name and discriminator (for identification)
  • Avatar URL: Your profile picture (to display your handsome face)
  • Server Information: Server ID, name, and member count (to function properly in your server)

2.2 Game and System Data

We collect and store data related to our various systems:

  • Pet System: Pet names, species, stats, abilities, equipment, inventory, battle history, training progress, and relationship data. (Your pet's soul is safe with us. Probably.)
  • Casino & Games: Currency balances, transaction history, game statistics, betting records, and tournament participation. (We track your wins and losses. Mostly losses. Let's be honest.)
  • Politics & War: Nation IDs, alliance affiliations, war statistics, and raid targets. (Public data from PnW API—we're not stalking you, the game is.)
  • Astrology: Birth dates (voluntarily provided) for zodiac and horoscope calculations. (We need your birthday to tell you your future. Trust us, we're professionals.)
  • Task System: Task completion history, rewards claimed, and daily/weekly progress. (We remember when you do your chores. Unlike your mother.)
  • Pet Stocks: Stock portfolio, transaction history, and investment performance. (We track your financial genius. Or lack thereof.)
  • Bazaar: Item listings, purchase history, and trading activity. (Your marketplace shenanigans are documented.)
  • Dungeon & Arena: Dungeon progress, battle records, and colosseum participation. (Your heroic failures are immortalized.)

2.3 Voluntary Information

You may voluntarily provide:

  • Birth dates for astrology features
  • Pet names and custom descriptions
  • Feedback and bug reports
  • Support requests (which we may or may not answer, depending on how chaotic things are)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To provide bot functionality across all systems (pets, casino, PnW tools, astrology, etc.)
  • Game Progress: To save and restore your game progress, stats, and achievements
  • Leaderboards & Statistics: To display rankings, leaderboards, and performance metrics
  • Notifications: To send DMs for alerts, beige expiry notifications, task reminders, and other requested notifications (only when you explicitly opt-in)
  • System Improvements: To analyze usage patterns and improve bot performance and features
  • Security: To detect and prevent abuse, exploits, and malicious activity
  • Support: To respond to your questions, issues, and support requests

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties (your soul is not for sale)
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Share your data with anyone except as required by law or to protect our services
  • Use your data for anything other than providing and improving our services

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in our secure databases located in the digital void. We implement reasonable security measures including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication for database access
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Protection against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we try our best. The Reaper takes security seriously.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your account is active and using our services
  • Inactive Accounts: Data may be retained for up to 2 years after last activity, after which it may be deleted or anonymized
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon request, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where required by law or for legitimate business purposes
  • Transaction Records: Financial and transaction records may be retained longer for security and audit purposes

6. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Discord: For bot hosting, user authentication, and API services. Discord's privacy policy applies to data shared with them.
  • Politics & War API: For retrieving public game data. This data is publicly available and not considered personal information.
  • Cloudflare: For web hosting and tunneling services. Their privacy policy applies to data processed through their infrastructure.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal and operational requirements)
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below. We'll respond within 30 days, unless the Reaper is busy collecting souls, in which case it might take a bit longer.

8. Cookies and Tracking

  • Authentication and session management

You can control cookies through your browser settings. (But disabling them might break things. The Reaper is not responsible for broken things caused by cookie paranoia.)

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this privacy policy.

11. Changes to This Policy
